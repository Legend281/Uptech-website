## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the cross-continental bridge between Silicon Mountain (Buea, Cameroon) and Texas enterprise corridors. It conveys absolute regulatory authority, engineering precision, and institutional reliability. The target audience spans diaspora entrepreneurs, multi-jurisdiction enterprise executives, and high-growth startups seeking formalization, staff augmentation, and offshore delivery.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate / Modern** clarity with technical precision. It rejects superficial minimalism in favor of structured density, tactile legitimacy, and institutional weight. Visual rhythm is achieved through deep oceanic navy depths contrasted against crisp architectural slate and luminous electric blue accents, anchored by restrained gold trust accents. Every viewport reinforces security, operational excellence, and global execution capacity.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate institutional atmosphere while signaling technological modernity:

- **Navy Primary (`#0B192C`) & Navy Secondary (`#1E3E62`)**: Serve as the foundational anchors for executive headers, mega navigation, structured footers, and authoritative callout containers.
- **Electric Blue (`#0066FF`) & Sky Precision (`#0284C7`)**: Signal technical rigor and digital capability. Employed for primary interactive states, progress bars, active filters, and interactive nodes.
- **Amber Accent (`#F59E0B`)**: Evokes stability, institutional accreditation, and cross-border regulatory certifications. Reserved strictly for trust seals, review ratings, compliance highlights, and key metric badges.
- **Surface Foundations (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`, `#FFFFFF`)**: Maintain expansive contrast, preventing dense corporate information from feeling claustrophobic.
- **Direct Engagement Green (`#25D366`)**: Dedicated exclusively to direct WhatsApp communication pathways, critical for West African and diaspora commercial workflows.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on a dual-engine structure:

1. **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Delivers geometric authority with sculptured modern numerals and confident title presentation. Tight tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) prevents loose reading paths on large corporate statements.
2. **Body & Utility (Inter)**: Handles micro-data, multi-column contract overviews, legal text, and operational workflow explanations with zero friction. Neutral x-height ensures cross-platform legibility in complex technical specifications.

Text elements intended for status indicators, country indicators (e.g., `USA HQ // CMR OFFICE`), or service category pills use `label-caps` in complete uppercase with expanded letter-spacing to reinforce institutional classification.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored within a maximum width of `1280px` for editorial balance and executive dashboards:

- **Desktop (≥ 1024px)**: 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, dynamic auto-margins. Primary sections observe a vertical cadence of 64px–96px between major content modules.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px safe side margins. Dual-split heroes stack naturally into balanced horizontal cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 16px lateral padding. Vertical section pacing tightens to 40px–48px.

### The Executive Dual-Split Hero Layout
The flagship landing component splits into two interactive columns:
- **Left Column (For Individuals / Expats / Professionals)**: Personal business formalization, visa tech vetting, diaspora investments.
- **Right Column (For Enterprises / Institutions)**: Managed offshore IT pods, corporate outsourcing, cross-border entity incorporation.

On desktop, both viewports stand side-by-side with subtle hover expansion (`flex: 1.15` transition). On mobile, the viewports pivot into a segmented switcher control at the top, immediately followed by the selected track's content card.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages crisp ambient shadows layered over architectural low-contrast borders:

- **Surface Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Neutral tint (`#F8FAFC`) serving as the background foundation.
- **Surface Level 1 (Resting Cards & Modules)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` background with a hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and subtle directional drop shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Segment Selectors)**: Raised elements step forward with an expanded shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.03)` with border color shifting to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Modals, Slide-overs, Floating Dual CTAs)**: Deep ambient containment: `0 20px 30px -10px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.16), 0 10px 15px -5px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08)`.
- **Deep Slate Overlays (Hero / Dark Corporate Shelves)**: Rich `#0B192C` surface containing subtle geometric technical grid lines traced in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, eliminating flat darkness.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft (`1`)** structural discipline for all structural elements, preserving architectural authority without aggressive brutality:

- **Cards, Panels, & Form Containers**: `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px) to provide balanced enclosure.
- **Input Fields & Standard Buttons**: `rounded-md` (0.375rem / 6px) for crisp interactive clarity.
- **Pill Tags, Service Flags, & Dual-Action FABs**: Fully rounded `rounded-full` (9999px) to establish deliberate contrast against rectangular content blocks.
- **Trust Badges & Metric Containers**: `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px) with inset 1px precision borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Book a Consultation")**: Deep Navy (`#0B192C`) background with crisp white typography, elevating to Electric Blue (`#0066FF`) on hover with a smooth 150ms ease. Features an embedded directional arrow icon. Height: 48px desktop / 44px mobile.
- **Direct WhatsApp CTA**: WhatsApp Green (`#25D366`) solid fill with white text and WhatsApp SVG icon. On hover: darkened green (`#1EBE5D`) with an ambient green focus ring (`rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary Corporate Button**: White background, 1.5px border in `#CBD5E1`, text in `#0B192C`. On hover: surface transitions to `#F1F5F9` with border in `#0B192C`.

### Dual-Split Hero
- **Structure**: Symmetrical split with subtle central divider. Left side defaults to Texas enterprise blue accents; right side features Buea innovation hub navy accents.
- **Card States**: Active side features an expanded subtle spotlight gradient (`radial-gradient(ellipse at top left, rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.06), transparent 70%)`).
- **Pathfinder Action**: Each track features targeted micro-actions (e.g., "Incorporate in Texas or Cameroon" vs. "Hire Vetted Dev Teams").

### Trust Badges & Accreditations
- Modular badge strip exhibiting:
  - **Jurisdiction Tokens**: Interleaved USA & Cameroon flags accompanied by registration codes (`LLC TX Verified`, `RCCM CMR Compliant`).
  - **Security & Compliance**: Inset pill design with amber checkmark icons (`#F59E0B`), micro uppercase labeling (`label-caps`), and frosted background tint (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08)`).

### Image-Rich Service Cards
- **Card Header**: 16:9 ratio imagery depicting real consulting engagements, cross-border offices, and software architecture schematics with a 10% dark navy duotone gradient overlay.
- **Body Content**: Service title in `headline-sm`, 2-line scope description in `body-sm`, followed by a cluster of pill tags.
- **Card Footer**: Clear text link with transition arrow: "Explore Scope & Deliverables →".

### Pill Tags & Category Chips
- Small height (26px), uppercase `label-caps`, `rounded-full`.
- **Neutral**: Slate background (`#F1F5F9`) with navy text (`#1E3E62`).
- **Highlight/Active**: Soft electric tint (`rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.10)`) with Electric Blue text (`#0066FF`).

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Internal padding: `12px 16px`.
- Focus State: Border color switches to `#0066FF` with a 3px outer glow ring (`rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.15)`). Label positioned outside top with required markers highlighted in corporate amber.
