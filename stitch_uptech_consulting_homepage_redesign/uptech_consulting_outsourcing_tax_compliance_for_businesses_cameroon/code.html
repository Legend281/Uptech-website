Direct Answers

Practical Tax Compliance Answers

Clear, unvarnished operational guidance on dealing with the Cameroon tax administration.

Missed declarations are common, particularly for companies operating during rapid growth or informal transition phases. Rather than ignoring the issue until a formal audit or account freeze occurs, UCO performs a discreet historical reconciliation. We recalculate your statutory liabilities, assemble the back-filings, and interact directly with your attached Tax Center (Centre des Impôts) to negotiate manageable settlement structures and penalty remissions where permitted by the General Tax Code.

When incorporating a new entity (SARL, SAS, or registered sole proprietorship), your statutory tax existence typically commences upon registration with the RCCM and the generation of your Unique Identification Number (NIU). However, if your business has transacted through registered corporate accounts prior to formalisation, clear documentation of founding capital is crucial. We help structure your company inception so that your opening balance sheet starts on solid, uncontested ground.

We recommend an automated cadence where your finance or operations lead uploads bank summaries, payroll sheets, and customer invoices to your secure client folder between the 1st and 5th of each month. This gives our desk 10 full working days to review ledgers, query any missing documentation, and lodge finalized returns well ahead of the official 15th monthly cut-off.

The Direction Générale des Impôts (DGI) falls under the Ministry of Finance and governs corporate income tax, withholding taxes (TSR), VAT/TVA, business licenses (Patente), and personal taxes at source. The Caisse Nationale de Prévoyance Sociale (CNPS) is Cameroon's national social security fund governing workplace accident insurance, pensions, and family welfare contributions. Both require monthly reporting, but they are audited and enforced by distinct state authorities. UCO provides integrated tracking across both registries.
